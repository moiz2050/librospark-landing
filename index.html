How it works

Read less. Remember more. Actually try it.

Each card holds one idea, explained in plain words, with a small action you can take the same day.

  1. Pick what you care about

    Choose topics like habits, focus, money or stoicism, and set a daily goal from 5 to 20 cards.

  2. Swipe through ideas

    Your feed puts your topics first. Double-tap to like, save the ones you want to keep, or open any book.

  3. Try one thing today

    Every idea ends with a tiny “try today” step, so learning turns into doing — and your streak grows.
