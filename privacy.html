Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

The short version

  • The Librospark app does not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. We do not have servers for the app at all.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your progress (likes, saves, streaks and settings) is stored only on your own device, and you can delete it at any time.
  • We only receive information about you if you choose to email us.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy explains how the Librospark mobile app (the “App”) and the website on which this policy is published (the “Website”) handle information. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of Librospark.

You can contact us about privacy at any time at hello@buildbyzero.com.

2. Information the App collects

We do not collect any personal data through the App. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not use your device’s advertising ID.

Information stored only on your device

To work, the App saves a small amount of information in its private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (see “Backups” below), and we cannot see it.

What is storedWhy
Cards you have liked and savedSo you can find them again in the Saved tab
Cards you have already seen, and how many you finished each dayTo show progress, your daily goal and your streak, and to show you new ideas first
Your settings: chosen topics, daily goal, text size and reminder timeTo personalise the App the way you asked

You can delete this information at any time by using Settings → Reset progress in the App, by clearing the App’s data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Device permissions

  • Notifications — only if you turn on daily reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device and contain the title of an idea from the App. No server is involved.
  • Run at start-up — so that reminders you have turned on continue after your device restarts.
  • Vibration — for small vibrations when you interact with cards.
  • Internet access — this permission is included by standard components of the App. The App’s content is built in and works offline, and the App does not use the internet to send any information about you.

3. Features that involve other services

Some features hand something over to another app or service, but only when you choose to use them. Those services handle information under their own privacy policies.

  • Sharing a card. When you tap Share, the App creates an image of the card and opens your device’s share menu. The image goes only to the app or person you choose.
  • Backups. When you save a backup, the App creates a file containing your likes, saves, progress and settings, and opens your device’s share menu so you can choose where to keep it (for example, your files or a cloud drive you use). We never receive this file. If you restore a backup, the file is read on your device only.
  • “Read the book” links. These open the Open Library website (run by the Internet Archive) in your browser, so you can find the full book. That website may collect information under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Rating the App. If you choose to rate the App, the rating window is provided by Google Play, and your review is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We only see reviews that you publish on Google Play.
  • Emailing us. If you contact us from the App or the Website, your email app sends the message.

4. Google Play

The App is distributed through Google Play. When you download or update the App, Google processes information under the Google Privacy Policy. Google provides developers with statistics such as the number of installs and, if you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data, technical crash reports (for example, device model, Android version and App version). These reports do not tell us who you are, and we use them only to fix problems in the App.

5. Information the Website collects

The Website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or third-party fonts or scripts. Like any website, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page you requested and the time of the request. These server logs are used only to deliver the Website and keep it secure, and are kept for a short period in line with the hosting provider’s standard practice.

6. When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and anything you write. We use this only to reply to you and to handle your request (for example, a question, feedback, or a request from a rights holder). We keep emails only as long as needed for that purpose and delete them on request. We never add you to a mailing list and never share your email with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

7. Children

Librospark is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. Because the App collects no personal data from anyone, it does not collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Legal bases for processing (UK and EU users)

Where the UK or EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on the following legal bases for the limited information described above:

  • Emails you send us: our legitimate interest in answering your messages (Article 6(1)(f)), or taking steps you have asked for.
  • Website server logs: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Information stored on your device by the App: this stays under your control and is not sent to us. It is stored only because it is strictly necessary to provide the features you use.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent. Because the App does not send us any personal data, the only personal data we may hold about you is in emails you have sent us. To use any of your rights, email hello@buildbyzero.com. We will respond within one month.

If you are in the UK or the EU, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office).

California and other US states: we do not sell or share personal information, we do not use it for targeted advertising, and we do not collect sensitive personal information.

10. Data sharing and international transfers

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone. Emails you send us are stored by our email service provider, which may store data in other countries. Where this applies, the provider uses appropriate safeguards required by law.

11. Security

The information the App saves is kept in the App’s private storage on your device, protected by your device’s own security. Because we do not collect your data, there is no central database about you that could be exposed. Please keep any backup files you create somewhere safe.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how Librospark handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date. If a change means the App would start collecting personal data, we will tell you in the App before that happens.

13. Contact

For any question about this policy or your privacy, email hello@buildbyzero.com.
